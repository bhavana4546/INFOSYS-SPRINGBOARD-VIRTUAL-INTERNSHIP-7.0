OpenCV captures frames, the Haar Cascade detects face-like regions, and the browser displays the processed stream and live metrics.

EVENTS

Event specification

The event log records camera connection and capture state changes, face appearance or disappearance, multiple faces, processing-mode changes, and test events. Every event belongs to one session; unchanged frames do not create duplicate events.

The newest 100 events per session are kept in memory and are cleared when the application restarts.

PRIVACY & LIMITATIONS

Detection, not recognition

This application detects face-shaped regions only. It does not identify people, save recordings, or send camera frames to external services. Use a camera only with appropriate permission and tell people when monitoring is active.

Lighting, camera angle, occlusion, resolution, and processing load can affect detection and frame rate. Results may include false detections or missed faces.
